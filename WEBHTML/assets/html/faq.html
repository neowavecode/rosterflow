Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

General Questions (All Users)

How do I change my profile (name, photo)?

From the main menu (drawer ☰), go to "Profile". Here you can tap on your profile picture to upload a new one and tap on the name field to edit it. Remember to hit save!

How do I change my password or email?

From the main menu (drawer ☰), go to "Settings". Here you will find separate options for "Change Password" and "Change Email". Follow the on-screen instructions.

How do I change the app's appearance (Dark/Light Mode)?

Go to "Settings" > "Appearance". You can choose from "Light Mode," "Dark Mode," or "Customizable" to select your own theme colors.

How do I change the language?

Go to "Settings" > "Language". You can select from over 30 available languages. The app will reload in your chosen language.

How do I export all my data?

Go to "Settings" and select "Export my data". The app will compile all your personal information, financial data (for Independents), or work records (for Employees) into a single JSON file for you to download. This is part of your Right to Data Portability.

How do I permanently delete my account?

Go to "Settings" and select "Delete my account". This is a permanent and irreversible action. All your personal data, including projects, invoices, or work history associated with your account, will be permanently deleted in accordance with our Privacy Policy. Please be 100% sure before proceeding.

For Independent Workers

What does the Dashboard show me?

The Dashboard is your financial command center. It contains 4 widgets:

  • Financial Summary: Shows your Income, Expenses, Pending Collection, and Net Profit for a selected date range.
  • Gross Income (6 Months): A bar chart showing your total income for the last 6 months.
  • Financial Overview (6 Months): A line chart comparing your monthly income, expenses, and net total.
  • Top Clients (6 Months): A pie chart showing which clients are your biggest source of income.
How do I add a new client?

From the main menu (drawer ☰), go to "Clients". Tap the floating (+) button to add a new client. You can add their name and, most importantly, their legal billing data (address, tax ID, etc.). This data will be automatically used when you generate an invoice for them.

How do I manage projects and tasks?

Projects are managed *within* each client. Go to "Clients", tap on a specific client, and you will see their project list. You can add new projects here. Tap on a project to enter its detail screen, where you can add, edit, and check off individual tasks for that project.

How do I create an invoice?

Go to a client's project list and tap the invoice icon (🧾) next to the project. The app will suggest creating an invoice from the project's *completed* tasks, but you are not limited to this. You can fully customize the invoice:

  • Add, edit, or delete any concept/line item.
  • Set the invoice number, emission date, and due date.
  • Add a custom tax percentage (%). The app will calculate the total for you.
  • Add your digital signature by signing with your finger in the designated box.
What is the invoice workflow? (Drafts, Sent, Paid)

This is the core of your accounting. The flow is designed to keep your dashboard accurate:

  1. Step 1: Save as Draft. After creating an invoice, tap the 3-dot menu (⋮) and choose "Save as Draft". The invoice is now saved, and its value starts appearing in your graphs as potential income.
  2. Step 2: Mark as Sent. Go to the "Invoices" section (from the main menu). In the "Drafts" tab, find your invoice, tap its 3-dot menu (⋮), and select "Mark as Sent". It will move to the "Sent" tab and will now be counted in your "Pending Collection" widget.
  3. Step 3: Mark as Paid. When your client pays you, find the invoice in the "Sent" tab, tap its 3-dot menu (⋮), and select "Mark as Paid". It will move to the "Paid" tab and will now be counted as "Income."

At any step, you can also tap the 3-dot menu to "Generate PDF" to save or send the file to your client.

How do I track my business expenses?

From the main menu (drawer ☰), go to "Expenses". Tap the floating (+) button to add a new expense. You can add the amount, description, and date. Most importantly, you can attach a photo of a ticket or receipt from your phone's gallery for your records.

How do I set my currency (e.g., $, €, £)?

Go to "Profile". You will see a "Currency" option where you can select from over 30 global currencies. This currency will be used across your dashboard and on all invoices you generate.

How do I remove the ads?

The Independent Worker mode is free and supported by ads. To remove them, tap the "Go Premium" button in the main menu. This will prompt a one-time, non-recurring payment processed securely through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store.

For Company Owners & Managers

What's the difference between 'Owner' and 'Manager' roles?

The Owner is the highest role. They create the organization, manage the subscription and billing, and can invite or remove anyone, including other Owners. The Manager has all the same daily operational powers as the Owner (create schedules, manage team, approve requests, etc.) but *cannot* manage billing or delete the organization.

How do I invite a new employee or manager?

Go to the "Team" tab. Tap the floating (+) button, enter the user's email, and select their role ('Manager' or 'Employee'). Tap "Generate Code". You must then copy this code and send it to your new team member (via text, email, etc.). They will need this code to register and join your specific organization.

How do I use the main Dashboard ("Panel")?

The "Panel" shows you who is working *today*. You will see a card for each employee with a shift:

  • Gray circle: The employee has not clocked in yet.
  • Green circle: The employee is currently clocked in.
  • GPS icon: Appears if you have required GPS for this employee's clock-in.
Tap on any card to see details: their planned shift, clock-in time, and (if GPS was active) a map screenshot of their clock-in location.

How do I create a new Schedule?

Go to the "Schedule" tab and tap the floating (+) button. You can give it a name (e.g., "October Week 3") and select the date range it applies to. It will first be saved as a "Draft."

What's the difference between a "Draft" and a "Published" schedule?

A "Draft" schedule is only visible to Owners and Managers. You can edit it freely. Employees cannot see it. A "Published" schedule is visible to all employees on the team. They will receive a notification and see it in their "My Schedule" calendar.

To edit a schedule, you must first "Unpublish" it, which makes it a draft again. Once your edits are done, you re-publish it.

How do the Schedule Templates work?

Templates save you time. First, create a "perfect" schedule in "Draft" or "Published" status. Then, tap the 3-dot menu (⋮) on that schedule and select "Save as Template".

The *next time* you create a new schedule, you can tap the "Load from Template" button. This will automatically fill in all the shifts and assignments, which you can then adjust as needed for the new week.

How do I require GPS for clock-in?

Go to the "Time Control" tab. You will see two switches:

  1. Activate Clock-in: This must be ON for employees to be able to clock in at all.
  2. Require GPS on Clock-in: If you turn this ON, the app will not let an employee clock in until they enable their phone's GPS, capturing their location at that moment.

How do I manage time-off or vacation requests?

All employee requests arrive in the "Requests" tab. In the "Pending" section, you can tap on each request to view the details and decide whether to Approve or Deny it. Once approved, the time off is automatically added to the employee's calendar. All managed requests move to the "History" tab.

Can I assign vacation or days off directly?

Yes. Go to the "Team" tab, tap on the employee's name, and select "Assign Vacation / Day Off". This allows you to add time off for them without requiring a request.

How do Announcements work?

Go to the "Announcements" tab. Create a new announcement (it's saved as a draft). When ready, tap "Publish" to send it to all employees. You can tap on a published announcement to see a read-status list (who has seen it and who has not).

How do I check my team's hours and export them?

Go to the "Records" tab. This is your team's complete time log. You can filter by date range to see a summary for each employee, including:

  • Planned Hours: Hours from their schedule.
  • Clocked Hours: Hours they actually clocked in/out for.
  • Balance: The difference (positive or negative) between planned and clocked hours.
You can tap the "Export to Excel" button to generate a spreadsheet for your payroll or records.

How does the 30-day free trial and subscription work?

When you create an organization, you get a 30-day free trial with 100% full access. After 30 days, a payment screen will appear. You must choose a subscription plan (e.g., Founder or Professional) to continue using the service. All payments are processed securely by Stripe.

How do I deactivate an employee who no longer works with me?

In the "Team" tab, tap on the employee's name. On their profile screen, you will find the "Deactivate Employee" option. The employee will lose access to the organization, but all their history (clocked hours, requests, etc.) will be preserved for your records.

For Employees

How do I register for the app?

You must get an Invitation Code from your Owner or Manager. Download the RosterFlow app, select "Register" as an Employee, and enter this code when prompted. This will automatically add you to your correct organization.

What's on my Home Screen?

Your home screen gives you a quick overview. You will see:

  • Notifications: For new chat messages, new announcements, or updates on your requests.
  • Current Week Widget: Shows your shifts for the week at a glance.
  • Next Shift Widget: Reminds you of your very next scheduled shift.
How do I clock in and out?

On your main screen, tap to enter your organization. Go to the "Clock-in" (Fichaje) tab. You will see a large button to "Clock In". Press it when you start work. The button will then change to "Clock Out," which you will press at the end of your shift.

Why does the app ask for my GPS? My clock-in button is disabled!

Your manager has activated the "Require GPS" feature. The app will not let you clock in until you enable location services (GPS) on your phone.

Your privacy is protected: the app only records your location one single time—at the exact moment you tap the "Clock In" button. It does *not* track your location before or after.

How do I see my upcoming shifts?

Inside your organization, go to the "My Schedule" tab. This shows a full monthly calendar. Days you work are marked. Tap on any day to see the detailed shift information: start time, end time, breaks, and any notes from your manager.

How do I request a day off or a shift change?

Go to the "Requests" tab. Tap the floating (+) button to create a new request. You can choose the type (e.g., day off, change shift) and select the dates. After sending, you can check its status (Pending, Approved, or Denied) in the "History" sub-tab.

How can I see my total worked hours?

From the main menu (drawer ☰), select "My Hours" (Horas Registradas). This screen shows you a detailed log of your planned shifts vs. the hours you actually clocked, including breaks.

How do I use the Chat?

Go to the "Chat" tab. You will automatically be in the main Group Chat for your whole organization. You can also tap the "private" icon or find a user's name to start a private, one-on-one chat with any co-worker or manager. Your private chats are not visible to anyone else.

I've read an announcement, can I view it again?

Yes. In the "Announcements" tab, new announcements appear in the "New" section. Once you read one, it moves to the "Viewed" section, where you can access and read it again at any time.
